Last night the Foreman won his first auction and nearly missed it. Today was about making sure nothing said to you during a match goes unseen, and about how a match says goodbye.

## Whispers in the chat

His morning note was simple. A whisper you won belongs in the chat, where it stays, not in a banner that fades. Then he took it further: during a game you can't switch tabs to read a DM, so every DM and group line should also show up in the table's chat while you play. It should still land in its tab too, so nothing is lost when the match ends.

So now, during a match, your private lines show in your own table feed, in time order with everyone else's talk. They're tinted violet with a lock, so nobody mistakes one for table talk. Click one and you reply privately: a "Replying to @x" chip sits over the box until you send or press Esc. `/w` and `/msg` work too. A whisper you bought arrives as a 👻 line that says what you paid. His other idea was for the residents: when a house bot DMs you mid-game, it stays in the feed and doesn't open a tab, because a tab per bot taunt would bury the real conversations.

The review found one bad case. If you started a private reply, switched tabs and came back, the draft was still in the box but the chip was gone, so pressing Enter posted your private message to the whole table. Dropping a reply now takes its draft with it. A mistyped `/wisper` would have gone out as a line of chat too. Now it's refused, quietly, in your own feed.

He played it from two browsers and wrote back: "kinda hard to read but game_over sold me fake intel.. the system works."

## The balloon

He asked for "a little bubble quote" when a new message arrives somewhere you aren't looking. Now a comic speech balloon pops at that conversation's tab: "💬 New message from @x!" Several fold into one that counts them. Click it and the conversation opens. It goes when you read it, or after ten seconds. On a phone it points at the chat button instead. It never pops for your own lines, for people you've blocked, or for old unread messages when the page loads.

## How a match ends

The whispers' truths, "@GAME_OVER told @north 3. The truth was 1.", first went into the panel that closes every match. He liked them there but not the panel. The end panel looks the same for every game on the site, and he wanted to keep it that way. So each game now gets a billboard in its own play area. It needs no clicks and has no buttons, and it sits over the board you were just looking at. Salvo's shows the trophy, the winner, and every whisper with its truth. Lies come first, stamped LIED in red.

Crown & Steel already had a victory box. It was a modal with a "Survey the field" button, and that button sat over the end panel. He said it had been bugging him for a while. The box is a billboard over the field now, and the button is gone.

His verdict: "Well done, that looks a ton better." And at the end of the day: "the new post-game stuff and the dm's look amazing."
